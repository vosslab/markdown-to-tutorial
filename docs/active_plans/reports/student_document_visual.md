# Student document visual acceptance review

Reviewed 2026-10-22 against the 12 final tutorial PDFs in
`/tmp/student_publishing_final_docs`. Status: **accepted for the requested
document-presentation work**.

## Evidence and method

The review rendered every page of all 12 already-built PDFs with `pdftoppm` at
105 dpi: 94 pages in total. A full collection contact sheet and the page images
are temporary review evidence under `/tmp/student_publishing_final_docs_visual/`.
No source, production output, or PDF was modified.

Detailed review included:

- `microbes_4_f_test`, page 3, for the F-statistic action and formula;
- `caffeine_3_quadratic_regression`, page 4, for the squared-column action,
  natural wrapped instruction, equation, and definition breakdown; and
- `microbes_5_anova`, page 2, for the ANOVA-table construction sequence.

## Findings

### Visual hierarchy and indentation

The collection has a consistent but not flat hierarchy. Major `Step` headings
begin farther left than the body text, while subordinate section headings use a
separate, intermediate placement. This makes action transitions easy to scan
without turning every heading into a large callout. The F-test and ANOVA pages
are particularly clear examples.

### Prose, equations, and actions

Narrative paragraphs that naturally run beyond two lines now have a clean,
consistent right edge. Their final lines remain naturally ragged, so they do
not look mechanically stretched. Explicit Markdown hard-break material stays
left aligned: the Caffeine equation and its individual definition lines retain
normal word spacing. The F-statistic formula is a distinct, editable-looking
green formula block rather than a formula hidden in a bullet. Inline formulas
remain restrained.

### Pagination and collection overview

The 94-page overview showed no clipped body text, missing images, overlap, or
orphaned headings at page edges. Images, figures, and tables remain legible and
fit inside the page area. Cover pages give the Caffeine and Microbes series
their intended visual personality while later instructional pages remain more
quiet and readable.

## Limits

This accepts the final PDF presentation for the requested heading indentation,
long-paragraph justification, hard-break behavior, formula prominence, and
page fit. It does not establish the appearance after Google Docs import, does
not test student comprehension, and does not compare against unavailable
original PDFs. The temporary rendered images are review evidence only.
