This is a direct source review of `tutorial_data/caffeine_1_descriptive_statistics.md`, `tutorial_data/caffeine_2_linear_regression.md`, and `tutorial_data/caffeine_3_quadratic_regression.md` against the read-only Caffeine outline and the restoration plan/report. ## Review method and limits

I used an expert cognitive walkthrough lens from the HCI skill: follow the learner's goal, identify the next action and its visible cue, and check that an action is not repeated or hidden in a second list. This is an expert inspection of authored source, not a participant study, usability test, or claim about student outcomes. The original outline remains the wording authority; cell references, formulas, data, and verified scientific corrections are valid reasons for source changes.
