# Babies tutorial teaching fixes

## Scope

Updated the three editable lessons after reviewing the findings in
[teaching_babies_review.md](teaching_babies_review.md) and reading the current student-facing
instructions. Preserved the fictional hospital stories, data tables, formula assignments, and
existing lesson sequence except where the timing of a hypothesis choice needed clarification.

## Changes

- In the Z-test and one-sample t-test lessons, identify Joe's right-tailed claim as the assigned
  test and require students to record that choice before calculating p-values. The lessons still
  calculate both tails and decisions for comparison, but now direct students to report only the
  prespecified test rather than select the smaller p-value.
- In the two-sample t-test lesson, move hypothesis selection ahead of the mean, standard deviation,
  and sample-size calculations. Students record the research question and hypotheses first, then
  use that choice when reporting results. This resolves the conflict between the calculation order
  and the later instruction to choose before looking at data.
- In the one-sample t-test setup, remove the unexplained option to copy tabs into a Part 1 workbook.
  The generated workbook is described as the complete standalone file for this lesson.

No data or spreadsheet formulas were changed. No build or LibreOffice export was run as part of
these source edits; the integrated rebuild and output review remain with the coordinating agent.
